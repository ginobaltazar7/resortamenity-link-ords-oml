Resort Amenity — Guest Brief Demo

A hospitality pre-arrival brief, composed from spend history. Staff-facing.

Source code: github.com/ginobaltazar7/resortamenity-link-ords-oml

SYNTHETIC demo data — not guest data. Every guest below is invented by data/generate_synthetic_data.py. There is no real person, stay, or prediction here.

How this works. Every number is computed from the sample CSVs — nothing is generated or guessed. Recommendations are rule-based: something new in the guest's top category, the most popular untried item, and a cross-sell, cross-type step-up; cold-start guests fall back to the most popular items overall. Remembered notes come from data/sample_guest_memory.csv — light hospitality preferences (ski, spa, onsen, dining, children), derived from purchase patterns or stated at check-in. Prep actions and suggested questions are fixed rules over those notes. We never show whether the guest later returned — that hasn't happened yet, and staff at arrival can't know it. The data column for it (actually_returned) exists for model training only, never for the brief. To run the same composer in Python, see data/memory_composer.py (it also prints this page's data with --demo-json). @ginobaltazar @getunkai 2026-10-27.
