ResortAmenity — Guest Brief Demo

A chain-recall-style brief, composed from spend history. Staff-facing.

SYNTHETIC demo data — not guest data. Every guest below is invented by data/generate_synthetic_data.py. Nothing here is a real person, a real stay, or a real prediction.

How this works. Every number is computed from the sample CSVs — nothing is generated or guessed. Recommendations are rule-based: something new in the guest's top category, the most popular untried item, and a cross-type step-up; cold-start guests fall back to the most popular items overall. The synthetic ground truth (actually_returned) is deliberately not used — it is future knowledge, and staff at arrival cannot know it. To run the same composer in Python, see data/memory_composer.py.
